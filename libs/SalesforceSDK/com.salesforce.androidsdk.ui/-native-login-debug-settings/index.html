NativeLoginDebugSettings

class NativeLoginDebugSettings

Debug-only choice between browser login and the SDK's built-in Native Login form, edited in the Login Options screen of the developer support menu and persisted across launches.

The settings are inert, and mode always reads AuthenticationMode.BROWSER, unless developer support is enabled and the app registered no Native Login activity of its own. When mode is AuthenticationMode.NATIVE, SalesforceSDKManager.loginActivityClass is DebugNativeLoginActivity.

The Native Login DPoP and hybrid choices live in config; they do not change SalesforceSDKManager.useDPoP or SalesforceSDKManager.useHybridAuthentication, which stay the browser login values.
